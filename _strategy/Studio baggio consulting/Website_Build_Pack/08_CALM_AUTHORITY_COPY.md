## Core Positioning

Calm Authority is a LinkedIn writing system for financial advisers.

It turns real adviser expertise into consistent, publish-ready LinkedIn posts in the adviser’s own voice.

It is built around a specific trust-market problem: financial advisers have valuable judgement, but too little of it is visible publicly before prospects, referral partners and centres of influence decide who to trust.

## Problem Copy

Financial advice is a relationship business, but too much adviser expertise is invisible until someone is already in the room.

There are 37,136 authorised financial advisers in the UK. Fewer than 300 post consistently on LinkedIn.

That creates a structural visibility gap.

Clients may trust adviser judgement in private, but prospects, referral partners and centres of influence often have no public evidence of that judgement before they choose who to speak to.

Generic AI makes the problem worse if it produces bland, template-like content. In trust-based markets, sounding the same as everyone else is a commercial risk.

Calm Authority was built to close that gap without asking advisers to write from scratch every week and without losing the voice, judgement or compliance posture that makes them credible.

## How It Works Copy

Calm Authority maps an adviser's real voice from writing samples, uses weekly research to surface relevant angles, and turns a URL, article or idea into draft LinkedIn posts.

The adviser stays in control. They review, edit and publish manually.

The system is designed to make consistency achievable without removing judgement from the process.

## Proof Points

Use proof carefully and avoid overclaiming.

Public proof from Calm Authority materials includes:

- one adviser grew impressions by more than 500% in a short measured period
- the press fact sheet cites +527% impressions in 28 days for one adviser
- the same fact sheet cites 52 inbound leads in six months for one IFA posting consistently
- the fact sheet cites two inbound LinkedIn enquiries in one month generating initial and ongoing revenue proof
- Money Marketing / Financial Times-related visibility study materials are referenced in Calm Authority materials

Do not invent proof.

Do not imply every adviser will get the same results.
