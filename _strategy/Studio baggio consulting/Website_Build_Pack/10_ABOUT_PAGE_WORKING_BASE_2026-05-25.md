# Studio Baggio About Page Working Base
*Created: 25 May 2026*

## Direction

The About page should be about Studio Baggio first, with Jayme as the named founder and strategic lead.

It should not make Studio Baggio sound like a small personal bio page or a vague service line. The strongest direction is:

- Studio Baggio is an applied AI systems studio.
- It builds practical AI systems for expert-led and trust-based businesses.
- Jayme leads the studio, but the work is strengthened by collaboration with industry experts, operators and subject-matter specialists.
- Calm Authority is the clearest proof point: a live product built with inside-market insight from a Chartered Financial Planner.
- The commercial promise is visibility, market understanding, demand capture and real ROI.
- The page should stay in third person.

## Strategic Page Shape

1. Studio Baggio exists to turn AI into practical commercial systems, not abstract AI advice.
2. Jayme's credibility comes from media, commercial storytelling, production pressure, brand, audience, marketing strategy and senior-stakeholder work.
3. The studio's credibility comes from live proof: Calm Authority, Business Tracker and client/product systems.
4. The page needs to show collaboration with industry experts clearly, because this stops Studio Baggio feeling like one person making generic AI tools.
5. The page should make the operating model clear: Jayme leads strategy and system design; subject-matter experts and client teams shape market truth; the output is a system that helps visibility, demand capture, lead intelligence or workflow.
6. The CTA should be advisory and selective, not mass-market.
