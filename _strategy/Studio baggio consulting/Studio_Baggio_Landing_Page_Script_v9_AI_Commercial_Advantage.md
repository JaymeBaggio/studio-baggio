# Studio Baggio Landing Page Script v9
*Created: 24 May 2026*
*Purpose: update v8 around the clearer AI commercial advantage positioning*

## Decision

v9 is clearer than v8 because it leads with the buyer's problem, not the internal framework.

The front-door message is no longer primarily "Growth Intelligence".

The front-door message is:

> Turn AI into a commercial advantage.

The buyer does not wake up wanting a diagnostic, a content system or a Growth Intelligence framework.

They know AI is changing how businesses compete. They need to know where AI can create real commercial value in their business, then they need somebody who can turn that into working systems.

That is the offer.

## Direct-Response Rule

The homepage has to do three jobs:

- Clarity: a serious buyer understands what Studio Baggio does within seconds.
- Conversion: each section moves them from "we should be using AI" to "this can help us compete better".
- SEO: the page uses plain-language terms around AI commercial advantage, AI strategy, AI search, SEO, market intelligence, lead capture, prospect research, authority systems and workflow acceleration.

The visual style can stay sparse, premium and editorial.

The message must be concrete.

## The Homepage Promise

Studio Baggio helps expert-led businesses find and build the AI systems that create commercial advantage.

Plain English:

> We help serious businesses work out where AI can make them easier to find, faster to act, better informed and harder to compete with.

## The Main Through Line

AI is not valuable because a business uses it.

AI is valuable when it improves how the business is found, how it learns, how it sells, how it serves clients or how quickly it acts.

The mechanism:

> Opportunity -> commercial use case -> proof of value -> practical system -> better visibility, intelligence, pipeline, workflow or client experience.

Growth Intelligence still matters, but it should sit under the hood as part of the method, not as the first thing the buyer has to understand.
