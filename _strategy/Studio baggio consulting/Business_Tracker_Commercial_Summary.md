# Business Tracker

## TL;DR

The Business Tracker helps businesses find, qualify and follow up with the right prospects before competitors know they are active.

It captures the actions people already take across the website, search, LinkedIn, email, lead magnets and referrals, then scores them against the business's own qualification criteria and turns them into named leads, market intelligence and clear follow-up.

## What it is

The Business Tracker is a market-specific prospecting system.

It is built around the market a business wants to win: who counts as a good prospect, what makes a lead valuable, which signals matter, which accounts to prioritise, what should disqualify someone, and what follow-up makes sense.

That means it can be adapted to any market. Financial advisers. Sports and entertainment clients. Private equity-backed firms. Founder-led businesses. Any audience where the question is: who should we be speaking to, why now, and what gives us a stronger reason to start the conversation?

## What it captures

Most businesses have useful prospect activity happening every week, but it is spread across too many places.

People visit the site. Find pages through Google or AI search. Read articles. Follow on LinkedIn. Comment on posts. Open emails. Click links. Download lead magnets. Fill in forms. Come through introducers.

The Business Tracker brings that activity into one commercial view.

It captures:

- website analytics, top pages and referrers
- SEO and AI search visibility
- LinkedIn followers, comments, engagement and top posts
- lead magnet downloads and diagnostic completions
- email opens, clicks and campaign activity
- enquiries, referrals and introducer routes
- priority accounts, decision-makers and market context

But it is not just a dashboard.

The point is to know who is showing interest, whether they meet your qualification criteria, what they care about and what to do next.

## The input map

Without the Business Tracker, these are separate touchpoints.

With it, every signal becomes business intelligence, a qualified prospect or a follow-up action.

### SEO and AI Search Visibility

Shows which pages attract the right visitors, which searches and AI answers you appear in, and which articles are creating follow-up opportunities.

Without lead capture, search is anonymous traffic.

### LinkedIn

Shows who followed, commented and engaged, which posts created useful attention, which formats performed best and which prospects are warming up.

Without tracking, LinkedIn is blind impressions.

### Lead Magnets

Shows who interacted, when they interacted, what they cared about, their lead quality score, the AI rationale and the best next follow-up.

Without email and follow-up, lead magnets are wasted contacts.

### Email Marketing

Shows behavioural triggers, source attribution, messaging stage, engagement score, lead quality, AI rationale and suggested follow-up.

Without the Business Tracker, email is sending without knowing who engaged.

## What it includes

### 01 Intelligence

Commercial insight your competitors do not have.

The Tracker shows which prospects, firms, accounts, introducers and market signals are becoming active before that activity turns into an obvious enquiry.

### 02 Attribution

Captures every interaction across the website, SEO, AI search, LinkedIn, lead magnets, email and referrals.

The business can see where interest came from, which content created it, which channel warmed it up and what happened next.

### 03 Qualification

Scores leads against the business's own criteria.

The rubric can include ICP fit, budget or funds, relevance, authority, relationship value, timing, source quality, engagement level and any other factor that matters in that market.

### 04 Live AI Search Intelligence

Adds current context from the market.

For one market, that might mean press, sponsorships, management changes, social signals or public activity. For another, it might mean funding news, hiring signals, leadership changes, regulation, partnerships, acquisitions, events or buying triggers.

The point is the same: the Tracker adds current market context to the lead record, so follow-up is based on what is happening now, not just what someone clicked last month.

### 05 Follow-up

Recommends the next action per lead.

The team can see who to prioritise, why they matter and what reason they have to start the conversation now.

### 06 Reporting & Scale

Creates a board-ready monthly view of what is working, what is not and what to optimise next.

It is designed to scale beyond the first use case: more advisers, more sectors, more target accounts, more campaigns, more data sources and more qualified opportunities over time.

## What it does with the data

Every named lead is qualified against the business's own criteria.

The Tracker shows where the lead came from, what they have engaged with, which touchpoints they have seen, how strong the opportunity is and why the AI has scored them that way.

It also suggests the next follow-up, so the team is not starting from a blank page or relying on memory.

A lead stops being a name in a spreadsheet. It becomes a clear prospect record: who they are, why they matter, what they have shown interest in and how to approach them.

## The Intel Bank

The Intel Bank adds the market layer.

It keeps target firms, decision-makers, introducers, recent news, campaign status, relationship context and follow-up angles in one place.

That gives the business information competitors do not have.

Who is active.
Who is warming up.
What has changed in the market.
Where the best opening is.
What reason you have to start the conversation.

## Why it matters

Prospecting works better when it is based on evidence.

SEO without lead capture is anonymous traffic. LinkedIn without tracking is attention without follow-up. Lead magnets without qualification are just names on a list. Email without attribution is guessing. CRM without market context is admin.

The Business Tracker connects those pieces.

For leadership, it shows where visibility is turning into opportunity.

For sales and marketing, it gives a clearer daily action list.

For the business, it creates a prospecting advantage: stronger lead capture, better market intelligence and a better chance of turning attention into live conversations.

Business intelligence. Qualified prospects. More conversations. More clients. More impact.
