# Business Tracker - Studio Baggio Site Copy

## Business Tracker

## Turn prospect activity into qualified follow-up.

Most businesses already have people showing interest.

They visit the site. Find articles through Google or AI search. Follow on LinkedIn. Comment on posts. Open emails. Click links. Download lead magnets. Fill in forms. Come through referrals.

But that activity usually disappears into separate tools.

The Business Tracker brings it into one commercial view, then turns it into named leads, qualification, market intelligence and suggested follow-up.

So your team can see who is warming up, why they matter, what they have engaged with and what to do next.

## Built around your market

The Business Tracker is built around the market you want to win.

Your ICP. Your target accounts. Your lead sources. Your content routes. Your qualification criteria. Your follow-up logic.

That means it can be adapted to any ICP, sector or offer.

The question is simple: who should we be speaking to, why now, and what do we know that gives us a stronger opening?

## What it captures

- website analytics, top pages and referrers
- SEO and AI search visibility
- LinkedIn followers, comments, engagement and top posts
- lead magnet downloads and diagnostic completions
- email opens, clicks and campaign activity
- enquiries, referrals and introducer routes
- priority accounts, decision-makers and market context

It is not a dashboard.

It is a prospecting system.

## The operating system

The Business Tracker captures every interaction, makes it visible and produces a named follow-up pipeline.

### 01 Intelligence

Commercial insight competitors do not have.

### 02 Attribution

Captures every interaction across website, SEO, LinkedIn, lead magnets, email and referrals.

### 03 Qualification

Scores leads against your own business criteria: ICP fit, funds or budget, relevance, timing, source quality, authority and relationship value.

### 04 Live AI Search Intelligence

Adds current context from the market around each lead or target account.

The source changes by market. For one business, that may be funding, hiring, regulation, acquisitions, partnerships, leadership changes or public commentary. For another, it may be agents, managers, press, sponsorships, injuries, tours, events, social activity or career moves.

### 05 Follow-up

Recommends the next action per lead, so the team knows who to prioritise and why.

### 06 Reporting and Scale

Creates a board-ready monthly view of what is working, what is not and what to optimise next. Designed to scale beyond the current set-up.

Delivers: visibility, qualified leads and commercial advantage.

## What it tracks

Without the Business Tracker, these are separate touchpoints.

With it, every interaction becomes business intelligence, a qualified prospect or a follow-up action.

### SEO and AI Search Visibility

- which articles convert, and where you sit in search
- AI search rationale
- optimal follow-up

SEO without lead capture is anonymous traffic.

### LinkedIn

- who followed, commented and engaged
- qualified prospects and best-performing formats
- when to post and what hooks work

LinkedIn without tracking is attention without follow-up.

### Lead Magnets

- who interacted, when and why
- engagement and lead quality score
- AI rationale and optimal follow-up

Lead magnets without qualification are just names on a list.

### Email Marketing

- behavioural triggers and source attribution
- messaging stage and engagement score
- lead quality, AI rationale and optimal follow-up

Email marketing without the Business Tracker is sending without knowing who you are sending to and who engaged.
