# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Leaders, marketers and advisers in UK financial advice and other relationship-led professional services. They need to understand whether their firm appears when buyers ask grounded AI search tools for guidance or recommendations, which competitors surface, and which evidence appears to influence the answer.

## Product Purpose

The Studio Baggio AI Discovery Benchmark is a dated, evidence-backed research product. It turns repeated AI-search testing into a clear market view, a transparent firm ranking and a commercial route into Studio Baggio's AI visibility audit. Success means a reader can understand the headline findings quickly, locate a firm, inspect its evidence and trust the limits of the method.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, commercially intelligent. The report should feel like Studio Baggio: quiet authority, editorial discipline and useful evidence without dashboard theatre.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards, live-market-feed styling or interfaces that imply continuous updates.
- Oversized metrics, decorative cards and ceremonial whitespace that delay the findings.
- Split-grid headings that strand labels on the left and push the useful content to the right.
- Method language presented before commercial meaning.
- Interactions that look clickable but do nothing.

## Design Principles

1. Put the finding before the method.
2. Keep one clear visual anchor in each viewport.
3. Use the shared Studio Baggio page grid, typography and restrained blue accent.
4. Make rankings interpretable in seconds; move engine and run detail into progressive disclosure.
5. Treat interaction reliability and evidence transparency as part of the brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, focus and keyboard use. Preserve semantic headings, visible labels, 44px minimum interactive targets, reduced-motion behavior and a no-JavaScript reading fallback. Tables and drawers must remain understandable without relying on color alone.
